# Retail Sales analysis using SQL

## Project Overview

This project analyzes the Sample Superstore dataset using SQL to understand sales performance, profitability, customer behavior, product performance, regional performance, and the impact of discounts.

The analysis covers 9,994 transactions and answers 10 key business questions to identify high-performing and under-performing areas of the business.

## Key Insights

### Company Performance
- Total sales : $2.30 Million
- Total profit : $286.4 Thousand
- Overall Profit Margin : 12.47%

### Regional Performance
- Left region generated the highest sales and achieved highest profit margin(15%).
- Central region achieved the lowest profit margin(8%).

### State Analysis
- California and New York together contributed to more than 50% of total company profit.
- 10 states generated overall losses.

### Category Analysis
- Technology contriuted for 36% of sales and 50% of profit.
- Furniture has 32% of sales but only 6% profit.  
- Office supplies contributed to 42% of profit.

### Customer Segment Analysis
- Consumer segment cntributed to 50% of sales and also achieved the highest profit.
- Corporate  Home Office segmants as well demonstrated healthy profitability.

### Discount Analysis
- Profitability decreases as the discount increases resulting in a negative profit margin.
- But Categorical analysis show some anomalies.
- Office Supplies receives the highest total discount (60.714), but still generates a relatively healthy profit of 42.77.
- Technology has the highest total profit (50.788) despite having the lowest total discount (15.656) among the three categories.
- Furniture is the most concerning category: despite having a lower total discount than Office Supplies, it generates only 6.443 profit, suggesting that factors beyond discount may also be affecting its profitability.

### Business Recommendations
- Investigate Furniture sub-category to identify products with recurring losses and reassess pricing strategies.
- Establish discount thresholds to prevent excessive discounting from eroding profit margins
- Increase focus on Technology products especially Copiers, Phones and Accessories, which are the strongest contributors to profitability.
