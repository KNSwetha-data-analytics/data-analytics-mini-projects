## Business Question 8

### Question
How does discount affect profit?

### Results
| Discount |  Total Sales | Total Profit | Profit Margin |
| -------: | -----------: | -----------: | ------------: |
|       0% | 1,087,908.47 |  320,987.603 |       29.505% |
|      ... |          ... |          ... |           ... |
|      80% |   16,963.756 |  -30,539.039 |     -180.025% |

- Categorical Analysis:
  
| Category        | Total Discount | Total Sales | Total Profit |
| --------------- | -------------: | ----------: | -----------: |
| Office Supplies |         60.714 |      31.301 |       42.770 |
| Furniture       |         23.630 |      32.300 |        6.443 |
| Technology      |         15.656 |      36.399 |       50.788 |

### Business Insight 
- Profitability decreases as the discount increases. At 0% discount, the business generates a profit of 320,987.603 with a healthy 29.5% profit margin.
- At the highest observed discount of 80%, the business incurs a loss of 30,539.039, resulting in a negative profit margin of -180.0%.
- This indicates that heavy discounting can significantly erode profitability and eventually turn profitable sales into losses.
- Office Supplies receives the highest total discount (60.714), but still generates a relatively healthy profit of 42.77.
- Technology has the highest total profit (50.788) despite having the lowest total discount (15.656) among the three categories.
- Furniture is the most concerning category: despite having a lower total discount than Office Supplies, it generates only 6.443 profit, suggesting that factors beyond discount may also be affecting its profitability.
- Therefore, discount alone does not explain all differences in profit across categories. Product pricing, cost structure, and category-specific margins may also play important roles.
